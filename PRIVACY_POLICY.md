# Privacy Policy

**Last Updated: January 30, 2026**

## Introduction

The Student Counseling App ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains how we handle information in our anonymous counseling service.

## Information Collection

### What We Collect
- **Anonymous User ID**: System-generated identifier (no personal info)
- **User Role**: Student, Psychiatrist, or Administrator
- **Communication Data**: Messages and session notes
- **Usage Data**: App usage patterns (anonymized)

### What We DON'T Collect
- Name
- Email address
- Phone number
- Student ID
- Device identifiers
- Location data
- Any personally identifiable information (PII)

## How We Use Information

1. **Service Delivery**
   - Facilitate anonymous counseling sessions
   - Match students with psychiatrists
   - Schedule follow-up sessions

2. **Communication**
   - Enable secure messaging
   - Send appointment reminders
   - Facilitate group sessions

3. **Service Improvement**
   - Analyze usage patterns (anonymously)
   - Improve app functionality
   - Ensure system security

## Data Storage and Security

### Storage
- All data stored in Firebase Cloud (Google Cloud Platform)
- Data encrypted in transit (TLS) and at rest
- Regular security audits

### Access Control
- Role-based access via Firebase Security Rules
- Minimum necessary access principle
- Audit logs for all access

### Data Retention
- Active session data: Duration of enrollment
- Inactive accounts: Deleted after 90 days
- Backups: 30-day retention

## Your Rights

### Anonymity
- Complete anonymity guaranteed
- No linkage to real-world identity
- No tracking across sessions

### Data Access
- View your data through the app
- Request data export
- Contact support for assistance

### Data Deletion
- Request account deletion anytime
- All associated data permanently deleted
- Confirmation provided

## Third-Party Services

### Firebase (Google)
- Backend infrastructure
- Firebase Privacy Policy applies
- GDPR compliant
- SOC 2 certified

### No Other Third Parties
- No analytics services
- No advertising networks
- No data sharing
